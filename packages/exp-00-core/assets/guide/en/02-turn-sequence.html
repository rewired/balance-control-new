Turn sequence and political actions

1. Place the drawn tile

The game draws a tile for you. Choose a highlighted empty hex that shares an edge with a tile on the board. You must place the tile if you can. Under the standard rules, the game discards a tile with no legal placement and tries another one. If the game runs out of tiles before you can place one, it skips your political action and settles the final round.

Check for Hotspots. If your placement fills the last of the six spaces around a Hotspot for the first time, the game checks the lead on that Hotspot before your political action. The sole leader moves one of their markers from supply onto the Hotspot, even if it is someone else’s turn. A tie or an empty supply for the leader means there is no payout. Each Hotspot gets one chance to pay out.

2. Take one political action

Inspect a tile to see the available actions for it. Choose one legal action and pay any required Resources from your supply to the bank. If an action is available, you must choose one. If none are available, the game completes the turn automatically.

Place or move Influence

  • Place one marker from your supply onto a tile for free.
  • Move one of your own markers already on the board to another tile. Count the shortest distance on the hex grid, including empty hexes. One step is free, two steps cost 1 Resource, and three steps cost 3. Choose any combination of Resource types. The maximum distance is three steps.

You can place or move onto tiles with opposing markers. The Start Committee is off limits for both actions.

Formalize Influence

Once every player has put each of their starting markers on the board at least once, you can create one new marker in your supply, up to your total marker limit. You can do this in either of two ways.

  • Standard Committee you control — pay 2 Resources of different types, for example 1 DOM and 1 FOR.
  • Start Committee — pay 1 DOM, 1 FOR, 1 INF and 1 extra Resource of any type. Each player may do this once per match. It requires no control.

Convert Resources

Use a Grassroots tile you control to trade 3 Resources of one type for 1 Resource of a different type. For example, trade 3 DOM for 1 INF. One action makes one trade.

Disrupt an opponent

  • Recall — once per round, pay 1 DOM, 1 FOR and 1 INF to return any one opposing marker on the board to its owner’s supply. The owner can place it again later. If it was a starting marker, its first placement still counts for Formalize Influence.
  • Counter-lobby — pay 2 Resources to suppress one opposing Lobbyist’s bonus on one standard Committee. The Committee must be within two hex steps of one of your real markers, adjacent to a Lobbyist controlled by an opponent, and free of any active Counter-lobby. The suppression applies only to that opponent’s bonus from that Lobbyist to that Committee. It ends when that opponent finishes their next turn, before any ensuing settlement.
  • Displace — once per round, pay 2 Resources to move an opposing marker. It must be on the same tile as one of your real markers or one step away. Its owner chooses where it goes, for free, before your turn ends. At least one tile next to the opposing marker’s current tile must be a legal destination. The Start Committee is excluded.
  • Damage — once per match, pay 3 Resources to permanently reduce production by 1 on an undamaged Resort controlled by an opponent. The Resort must be within two steps of one of your real markers.

For Counter-lobby, Displace and Damage, you can pay in any combination of Resource types. Counter-lobby, Displace and Damage measure distance as a shortest path on the hex grid, including empty hexes.
