Production and end of the game

Settle the round

After everyone has taken one turn, the game settles the round. Each Resort produces Resources of its own type from the bank. Start with its printed W value. In CORE, a damaged Resort produces one less, with a minimum output of zero. Then use the Influence totals on that tile, including any Lobbyist bonuses, to distribute the output.

  • One leader receives the full output.
  • A tie for a positive lead gives each tied leader an equal whole-number share. Any remainder goes to Noise. Players below the lead receive nothing.
  • All totals zero means the Resort produces nothing, even for Noise.

Example. Red and Blue each have 2 Influence on an undamaged DOM W5 Resort. Green has 1. Red and Blue receive 2 DOM each, Green receives none, and 1 DOM goes to Noise.

After production, each player keeps up to 12 Resources of each type in their supply. Any excess goes to Noise. For example, if you have 11 DOM and receive 3 more, keep 12 and move the other 2 to Noise.

Win by reaching the marker limit

As soon as a player reaches 7 real markers on the board with 2–4 players, or 8 with 5–6 players, finish the effect that placed the last required marker. That player wins immediately, before any further Hotspot payout, political action or round settlement. If the same effect brings several players to their limit, they share the win. A Hotspot can end the game on another player’s turn.

Win when the tiles run out

If nobody reaches the marker limit, finish the game when there are no more tiles to draw.

  • If the last drawn tile was placed, finish its Hotspot payouts and the active player’s political action phase.
  • If the draw attempts ended without placing a tile, skip that turn’s political action. With Tile recycling, this can happen with unplaceable tiles still in the pile.

Then settle the round once, even if some players have not taken a turn that round. Count the real markers each player has on the board. The highest count wins, and players tied for the highest count share the win.
