Overview

Build a shared map of hex tiles and compete to get your Influence markers onto it. Control of a tile can earn you Resources or let you take a special action. Resources help you create and move markers. Your score is the number of your real markers on the board.

Your first round

  1. On your turn, place the drawn tile in an empty hex next to a tile already on the board.
  2. Take one political action. For example, place a marker from your supply on a Resort tile to compete for its production.
  3. After everyone has taken a turn, the Resorts produce. At the end of the round, you receive Resources based on where you lead or share the lead.

Once everyone has placed all their starting markers at least once, you can spend Resources at a Committee to create more. Then use an action or a Hotspot payout to put those markers on the board.

How you win

Reach 7 markers on the board with 2–4 players, or 8 with 5–6 players, to win immediately after the effect finishes. If the draw pile runs out first, the game finishes with one last round settlement and the players with the most markers on the board win.
