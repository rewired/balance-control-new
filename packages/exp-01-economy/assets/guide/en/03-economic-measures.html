Economic measures

Take and play Measures

Open Measures during a match to see each active expansion’s market, with up to three face-up cards per deck. Across all decks, you can hold two cards and play one card per round.

  1. Take Measure. Take one face-up card and the game refills that market. The base cost is zero. In a new match, if your whole hand was empty and you take in your regular action, you still get that action. You may play the new card immediately if legal. You cannot take a second card that turn. Taking with a card already in hand or as an additional action ends your turn.
  2. Play Measure. Choose a card from your hand and pay its full cost. In a new match, the game deducts one Resource of the card’s own deck type from its printed price before adding other costs. For example, Economic Stimulus has a printed price of ECO plus FOR, so its card cost starts at 1 FOR.
  3. Take your additional action. After the card resolves or sets up a future effect, the market refills and you take one other legal political action. You cannot play a second Measure in that slot. If no action is legal, the game closes the slot.

After its first play, a card can return when its deck needs a refill. After its second play, it is discarded permanently. An effect already in play lasts until its own timing or trigger ends it, even if the card has moved.

Choose a Measure

  • Economic Stimulus doubles a Resort’s printed value at the upcoming settlement. Location Debate reduces any chosen Resort’s output by 1 at that settlement. Subsidy Reduction does the same to an ECO Resort controlled by an opponent when you choose it.
  • Investment Freeze targets an opponent. At the upcoming settlement, ignore Measure-based production modifiers on Resorts that opponent controls when they produce. Damage and Regulations still apply. A tied Resort is outside the freeze.
  • Budget Compromise prevents a chosen Hotspot’s payout if it is first surrounded this round. The Hotspot’s chance is spent even though no payout occurs.
  • Collective Bargaining stops trades out of ECO this round, including the Investment Program trade. Debt Brake stops Convert Resources for everyone in the next round.
  • Budget Deficit adds 1 Resource to a chosen player’s next political action. It is spent on that attempt, including when the surcharge leaves the player with no legal action in the slot.
  • Economic Council lets you pay one non-ECO Resource in place of one ECO once this round. Supplemental Budget lets you pay one Resource of your choice in place of one eligible Measure or Regulation surcharge on a later action this turn.

Timing. An effect for this round includes the upcoming settlement. An effect for next round starts when the next round begins, after the current settlement. A card for the next settlement is played in your action window and takes effect at that settlement.
