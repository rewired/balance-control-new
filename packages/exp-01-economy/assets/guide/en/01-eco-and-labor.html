ECO and the labor market

Earn and spend ECO

ECO stands for Economy. It is a distinct Resource type. For example, ECO and DOM satisfy the two-different-types cost at a standard Committee. ECO also counts toward costs that accept any Resource.

ECO Resorts produce ECO at round settlement. Their W number gives the starting output. For example, if you lead alone on an unmodified ECO W3, you receive 3 ECO. If two players tie for a positive lead, each receives 1 ECO and the remaining 1 goes to Noise.

Change a Resort’s output

Start with the printed value, apply any permitted doubling, then add increases and subtract reductions. Bring the final output up to zero if it went below zero. Only then work out who receives it.

Example. Economic Stimulus and Location Debate both affect a W3 Resort at the upcoming settlement. Double 3 to 6 first, then subtract 1. The output to distribute is 5.

Surround the Labor Market

The Labor Market is a Hotspot. When all six neighboring spaces fill for the first time, the sole leader puts one marker from their supply onto it. A tie or an empty supply means no payout. That first enclosure is the only chance to collect the marker. The Labor Market produces no Resources.
