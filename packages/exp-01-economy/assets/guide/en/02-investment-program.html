Investment Program

Control both the Investment Program and a Grassroots tile to unlock a cheaper trade. Choose Convert Resources at that Grassroots and exchange 2 ECO for 1 Resource of another active type. The two tiles can be anywhere on the board. Virtual Influence can give you control of either tile.

Example. You control both tiles and have 2 ECO. You can trade them for 1 INF. You can also choose the ordinary Grassroots exchange if you have its Resources. You get one trade for the action.

Where the action happens

The Grassroots is the tile where you execute the trade. Its restrictions, surcharges and output modifiers apply. The Investment Program gives you access to the trade even if a Blockade is attached to the Program itself. A Blockade on the chosen Grassroots prevents the trade.

With Security & Order active, the Investment Program trade into SEC still gives 1 SEC before modifiers. The 2 SEC output is for the ordinary exchange. If Security Level on the Grassroots reduces your Investment Program output to zero, a legal trade still uses your action and costs the full payment.
