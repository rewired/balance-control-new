Overview

Economy & Labor adds ECO, a Resource you can produce and spend, and gives you more ways to exchange Resources and influence the next round settlement.

What goes into the match

Enable Economy & Labor under Expansions. The game adds ECO Resorts, the Labor Market Hotspot and the Investment Program to the tile pile. The Economy deck starts with three face-up Measures in its market. Your hand starts empty.

Your first decisions

  1. Compete for an ECO Resort if you want ECO from settlement.
  2. Look for a Grassroots tile and the Investment Program if you want to trade ECO for a Resource you lack.
  3. Look at the Measure market before choosing your political action. A Measure can change what a tile produces or what an action costs.
