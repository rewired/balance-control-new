Countdowns

Countdowns are reserved in this rules version. There are no Countdown markers to place, advance or resolve. The reserved cards Transformation Blockade and Future Committee are left out of the deck.

Play Transformation Pressure as a Hotspot

When all six spaces around Transformation Pressure first fill, check who leads on it. The sole leader moves one real Influence marker from their supply onto the Hotspot if the payout is allowed. A tie, an empty supply or an effect that prevents the payout still uses up this first enclosure.

Example. Blue leads when Red fills the last open space next to Transformation Pressure. If Blue has a marker in supply and no effect prevents or reduces the payout, Blue places one marker on the Hotspot before Red’s political action. Nothing counts down at settlement.

Follow each Measure’s timing

  • This turn ends before settlement. This round includes the current round settlement.
  • Until the beginning of your next turn starts when the card resolves and ends before your next draw. It can cover other players’ turns in between.
  • During the target player’s next turn starts when that player next begins a turn. Until the end of the next round starts now and includes the next round’s settlement if that round occurs.
  • Until consumed ends when the card’s stated event uses the effect.
