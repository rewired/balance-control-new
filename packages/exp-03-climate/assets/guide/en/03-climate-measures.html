Climate measures

Take and play Measures

Open Measures and choose a face-up card in the Climate market. The base cost to take it is zero and the game refills the market immediately. In a new match, taking with an empty hand in your regular action window leaves that action available. You may play the card immediately if legal. You cannot take a second card that turn. Taking with a card in hand or in an additional action ends your turn.

You can hold two Measures and play one per round across all active decks. In a new match, subtract 1 CLM from the printed price of a Climate Measure before adding other costs. After a successful play, take one other legal political action if available. A card can return after its first play. Its second play discards it permanently, and its effects continue for their stated duration.

Deck setup. Energy Crisis, Technology Initiative and Supply Chain Disruption require Economy & Labor. With those cards the Climate deck starts with 13 cards. Without them it starts with 10.

Tax an action or limit a gain

  • Transformation Directive adds 1 CLM or 1 DOM to actions on one chosen Resort tile until your next turn begins. Carbon Levy does so on all Resort tiles of one chosen type through the end of next round. Only one Carbon Levy at a time can target that type.
  • Extreme Weather Event adds 1 CLM or 1 DOM to placing a Resort tile until your next turn begins. If no placement can be paid for, discard the tile and draw again if possible, even with Tile recycling. Energy Crisis instead adds 1 DOM to Convert Resources until your next turn begins.
  • Future Investment gives you 1 Resource of a chosen Resort’s type immediately. Future Resolution prevents a chosen player from playing a Measure during their next turn.
  • Greenwashing blocks ordinary Hotspot Influence payouts and standard Committee formalization until your next turn begins. The player who would gain the marker can pay a fixed 1 CLM or 1 INF to lift that restriction for the gain if it is otherwise allowed. The fee applies once per gain, even if several Greenwashing effects apply. The Start Committee is immune.
  • Protest Movement blocks ordinary Hotspot Influence payouts until your next turn begins. It has no fee to lift the restriction, so paying for Greenwashing cannot restore a payout that Protest Movement blocks.
  • Supply Chain Disruption limits one player to 1 Resource from each producing Resort until your next turn begins. Calculate the regular shares and Noise remainder first. Cancel only the excess from the chosen player’s share.

Example. A W5 Resort with no output modifiers has two tied leaders. Each would receive 2, with 1 going to Noise. If one leader is affected by Supply Chain Disruption, they get 1, the other still gets 2 and Noise still gets 1. The bank does not pay the canceled unit.

Waive or transfer a surcharge

  • Adaptation Strategy waives eligible Climate surcharges for everyone until your next turn begins.
  • Technology Initiative waives eligible Climate surcharges for your additional political action after playing that card.
  • Future Pact waives eligible Climate surcharges originally assigned to you until your next turn begins.
  • Intergenerational Pact lets you assign one eligible surcharge to another player. They must pay it if the action resolves. If anyone cannot pay their full share, the action fails without payment. The Pact is used up only on a successful transfer.
