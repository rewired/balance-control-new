Overview

Climate & Future adds CLM and Measures that can change what an action costs, prevent certain Influence gains or protect players from Climate surcharges.

What goes into the match

Enable Climate & Future under Expansions. The game adds CLM Resorts and one Transformation Pressure Hotspot to the tile pile. The Climate deck starts with three face-up Measures in its market. Some Climate cards enter the deck only when Economy & Labor is also active.

Your first decisions

  1. Lead on a CLM Resort to earn CLM at settlement.
  2. Before moving a marker or placing a Resort, check whether a Climate Measure adds a cost. Some ask you to pay CLM or DOM.
  3. Watch who leads on Transformation Pressure. It pays out Influence when first surrounded, like a CORE Hotspot.
