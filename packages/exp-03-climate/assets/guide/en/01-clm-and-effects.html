CLM and climate effects

Earn and spend CLM

CLM stands for Climate. You earn it from CLM Resorts at round settlement. A CLM W2 starts with an output of 2 CLM. Apply any production modifiers, then distribute the output according to the CORE majority rules. CLM counts as a distinct type when you need different Resources. You can also use it in a payment that accepts any type.

Recognize a Climate surcharge

A Climate effect comes from a Climate Measure, a rule that requires CLM as a printed base cost, or a rule that explicitly calls it a Climate effect. What matters is where the cost came from. Paying a Carbon Levy with DOM still pays a Climate surcharge. Choosing CLM for an Administration surcharge leaves it a Regulation surcharge.

Example. An INF Resort has Administration and is subject to Carbon Levy on INF. Moving one step onto that Resort costs 1 Resource of any type for Administration plus 1 CLM or 1 DOM for Carbon Levy. With a Climate waiver that covers your move, pay only the Administration surcharge. If the destination has a Blockade, the move is prohibited before either cost applies.

Work out the final payment

  1. Apply Start Committee immunity where it applies, then check prohibitions.
  2. Collect the costs. Apply eligible Climate waivers to additional costs from Climate effects. Base prices and fixed payments such as Greenwashing’s fee still apply.
  3. With Supplemental Budget, you can replace an eligible remaining surcharge with its fixed payment. With Intergenerational Pact, you can then transfer an eligible remaining surcharge. Waived costs and fixed replacement payments cannot be transferred.
  4. Check that every payer can cover their full share before resolving the action. Personal Climate protection uses the original payer, even if someone else later pays a transferred cost.
