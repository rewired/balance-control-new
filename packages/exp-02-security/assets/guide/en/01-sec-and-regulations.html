SEC and regulations

Get and spend SEC

SEC stands for Security. It counts as its own type when a payment requires different Resources. SEC Resorts produce at round settlement under the standard production rules. You can also trade 3 DOM for 1 SEC at a Grassroots you control, just as you would trade for another Resource.

Placing a Regulation normally costs 1 SEC before modifiers in a new match. Moving one normally has no base cost. Regulations stay on their tiles until an effect moves or removes them. They can stack and affect all players, regardless of who placed them. Removing one returns it to the supply without a refund.

Four kinds of Regulation

  • Security Level reduces eligible output by 1. This includes Resort production, Grassroots trades, new Influence from formalization and ordinary Hotspot payouts.
  • Control adds 1 Resource of any type to an eligible payment. Attach it to a tile that neither produces nor converts Resources, such as a standard Committee.
  • Administration adds 1 Resource of any type to an eligible payment. Attach it to a tile that produces or converts Resources, such as a Resort or Grassroots.
  • Blockade prevents effects that resolve on its tile. See Blockades and resolution order for how to tell where an effect resolves.

The Start Committee is immune to Regulations. Elsewhere, Regulations can change what an effect does while leaving Influence totals and control of the tile as they were.

Inner Order and Authority Apparatus

When Inner Order is first surrounded, the sole leader may place one Regulation or move an existing one. This Hotspot’s reward is the Regulation operation. The leader pays its full cost, and the effect resolves at Inner Order even when the Regulation goes on another tile.

If you control the Authority Apparatus, you can use a political action there to move or remove one Regulation anywhere on the board. In a new match you may instead place a Security Level, Control or Administration at the normal placement price. Apply local cost and effect rules at the Apparatus. The Regulation and its destination still have to satisfy their own restrictions.
