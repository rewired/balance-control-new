Security measures

Take and play Measures

Open Measures and choose a face-up card in the Security market. The base cost to take it is zero and the game refills the market immediately. In a new match, taking with an empty hand in your regular action window leaves that action available. You may play the card immediately if legal. You cannot take a second card that turn. Taking with a card in hand or in an additional action ends your turn.

You can hold two Measures and play one per round across all active decks. In a new match, subtract 1 SEC from the printed price of a Security Measure before adding operation costs and other modifiers. After a successful play, take one other legal political action if available. A card can return after its first play. Its second play discards it permanently, and its effects continue for their stated duration.

Place, move or remove a Regulation

  • Threat Situation places one Regulation immediately. Jurisdiction Shift moves one. Legal Review removes one.
  • Emergency Decree names a Hotspot before it is surrounded. When it first encloses, you attempt to place a Blockade before the majority check. Pay for the placement at that moment. The card’s effect is spent on that attempt, even if the placement fails.
  • Deployment Order lets you move the next Regulation you successfully place. You get one chance to move that same Regulation. If you decline or cannot move it, it stays where you placed it.
  • Competence Conflict lets you pay to place Control on the Authority Apparatus after your next successful activation there this turn. That placement is optional and has its own cost.
  • De-escalation removes all Regulations from a Hotspot before production at the upcoming settlement. Loss of Control instead places one on a Hotspot at that time, with the placement cost due then.

Example. In a new match, Threat Situation’s printed SEC plus INF becomes 1 INF for the card. Add 1 SEC for its immediate placement, then apply any other costs to the combined payment. Emergency Decree charges its card cost when you play it and checks the placement cost separately when the chosen Hotspot is enclosed.

Change costs or restrict choices

  • Order Partnership lets you pay SEC in place of one Resource when paying for a Regulation or Measure this round. Situation Assessment reduces the next placement by 1 SEC this round, down to a base of zero in a new match.
  • Parliamentary Oversight makes each applicable Regulation add another 1 Resource to eligible costs this round. State of Exception adds 1 Resource to each player-paid resolution this round.
  • Risk Address prevents a chosen player from playing Measures next round. Surveillance keeps Regulations on the chosen tile from being moved or removed this round. File Status makes a chosen Regulation count twice when stacking its effect this round.
