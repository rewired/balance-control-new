Overview

Security & Order adds SEC and Regulations that attach to tiles. You can use them to raise action costs, reduce payouts or shut down effects on a tile.

What goes into the match

Enable Security & Order under Expansions. The tile pile gains one Inner Order Hotspot, one Authority Apparatus and three SEC Resorts with printed values 1, 1 and 2. With five or six players, add a second SEC Resort with printed value 2. The Security market starts with three face-up Measures from its own deck.

Your first decisions

  1. Control a SEC Resort to produce SEC at settlement, or use a controlled Grassroots tile to trade for SEC.
  2. Watch who leads on Inner Order as its last neighboring spaces fill. The leader can use that moment to place or move a Regulation.
  3. Control the Authority Apparatus or use a Measure to change which Regulations are on the board.
