# ERRATA-0037 — Measure and Regulation access

Date: 2026-11-10

Applies to EXP-01-06 and EXP-02-01/-04/-05/-06/-08-M15. The shared Measure rules apply to every enabled deck, including EXP-02 and EXP-03 without EXP-01.

An empty-handed player may take one Measure in their regular Political Action window and still perform their regular Political Action. This can be PlayMeasure, including the card just taken. A second TakeMeasure in that turn is unavailable after using the empty-hand pickup. The normal hand limit, play limit, acquisition payment modifiers and market refill still apply.

Reduce the printed cost of a Measure by one unit of its deck's resource (ECO, SEC or CLM), before adding operation costs and other modifiers. A single-unit printed cost can become zero.

An ordinary Grassroots exchange into SEC yields 2 SEC. Regulation placement costs 1 SEC before modifiers. Situation Assessment can reduce that base to 0. A controlled Authority Apparatus can also place SecurityLevel, Control or Administration as its Political Action, with the normal placement price.

New matches with a Measure deck record these rules at setup. Saved matches and replays without the rule marker retain their recorded prices, outputs and action timing.
