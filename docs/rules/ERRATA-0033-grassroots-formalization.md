# ERRATA-0033 — Neutral Grassroots and conversion-assisted formalization

Date: 2026-11-09
Status: Implemented
Applies to: CORE-01-02-07, CORE-01-04-19A through -19D, CORE-01-04-21A, CORE-01-04-22D, CORE-01-06-00-05C/-08, EXP-01-05, EXP-01-08-M07-04, and EXP-02-04-B-05

The active player can exchange Resources at a Grassroots they control or one with no controller. The ordinary rate is three Resources of one active resort for one Resource of another. The Investment Program alternative remains available to a player who controls both required Tiles.

The active player can include one exchange in a FormalizeInfluence action. They spend the output on that formalization and create one Influence. Plan the exchange at the Grassroots and then the formalization at the Committee. Apply local restrictions and costs at each Tile, share action-wide limits across the two effects, and commit both together.

## Worked cases

1. A has 4 INF and controls a Standard Committee. A uses a neutral Grassroots to exchange 3 INF for 1 DOM, then spends that DOM and the remaining INF to formalize. This uses one Political Action.
2. With 3 INF and no other Resources, A can choose the standalone exchange. A cannot combine it with that formalization because the exchange would leave only 1 DOM to spend.
3. A virtual-only majority for B makes the Grassroots unavailable to A. A tie makes it neutral.
4. A cannot use a blockaded Grassroots for the combined action even if the chosen Committee is the Start Committee. The Start Committee's immunity applies to the formalization at that Committee.
5. A SecurityLevel that reduces either output to zero makes the combined option unavailable. A separate otherwise legal and fully payable zero-output exchange or formalization remains available under EXP-02-04-B-05.

## Playtest scope

The paired playtest found the clearest change when a player could combine the effects and access neutral Grassroots. Its late production reduction, 2:1 ordinary rate, and targeted Standard Committee surcharge were experimental settings. The rule applies whenever the underlying actions are eligible.
