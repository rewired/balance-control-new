# ERRATA-0036 — Influence distance and disruption actions

Date: 2026-11-10
Status: Implemented
Applies to: CORE-01-00-T06, CORE-01-04-09, -12A/-12B, -27 through -34, CORE-01-05-04, EXP-02-02-B-02B

Ordinary Influence movement costs zero, one, or three Resources for one, two, or three hex steps respectively. It has a maximum distance of three hex steps. Placement from PersonalSupply follows CORE-01-04-11.

CounterLobby, DisplaceInfluence, and DamageResort are standard Political Actions. The acting player may choose any active resorts for their base payments. CounterLobby temporarily suppresses one Lobbyist contribution to a standard Committee. DisplaceInfluence gives the opposing marker's owner the choice of eligible adjacent destination. DamageResort adds a permanent production modifier of -1 to an eligible opposing ResortTile.

New matches record both rules at setup. Saved matches and replays use the rules recorded at their own setup, including the settings of the earlier playtests.
