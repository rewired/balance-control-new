# ERRATA-0035 — Influence recall

Date: 2026-11-10
Status: Implemented
Applies to: CORE-01-04-09 and -23 through -26, CORE-01-08-02, EXP-01-06-01, EXP-02-02-B-02B

RecallInfluence is a standard Political Action. Once per round, a player may pay one Resource from each of three different active resorts to return one real opposing Influence marker to its owner's PersonalSupply. The chosen marker's Tile is the ContextTile. Apply its prohibitions and payment modifiers. The Start Committee is excluded.

The owner may place the returned marker again through PlaceOrMoveInfluence. Recalling a Starting Influence marker preserves its first placement for the formalization timing restriction.

New matches record the rule at setup. Saved matches and replays use the rules recorded at their own setup.
