# ERRATA-0032 — Victory at the Influence limit

Date: 2026-11-09
Status: Accepted by the user
Applies to: CORE-01-06-03 and CORE-01-09

The first player to place their full allotment of real Influence on Board wins after the effect that placed the last required marker finishes. The limit is seven in CORE and eight with the 5–6 Player Add-On. Influence in PersonalSupply and virtual Influence do not count.

If an enclosure award reaches the limit, complete that enclosure event and end the game before any later enclosure event or Political Action. The win takes precedence over the final Round Settlement even if the placement used the last Tile. If nobody reaches the limit, exhaustion still leads to the final Round Settlement, followed by the usual comparison of real Board Influence and shared victory on ties.
